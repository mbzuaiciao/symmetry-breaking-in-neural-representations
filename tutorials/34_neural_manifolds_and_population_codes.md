# Tutorial 34: Neural Manifolds and Biological Population Codes

## 1. Population-Level Representation Geometry

In motor cortex (M1), prefrontal cortex (PFC), and hippocampus, single-neuron recordings exhibit high variability and complex heterogeneous tuning. However, at the **population level** (simultaneous multi-electrode recording of hundreds of neurons), activity is constrained to low-dimensional neural manifolds.

---

## 2. Shared Latent Dynamics Across Individuals

- **Rotational Dynamics in Motor Cortex (jPCA)**: During reaching movements, motor cortical trajectories form smooth rotational orbits in a low-dimensional plane.
- **Cross-Subject Alignment**: Even though neuron-to-neuron mappings between two primates cannot be established, applying Orthogonal Procrustes / Canonical Correlation to their population state trajectories reveals nearly identical low-dimensional manifolds.
